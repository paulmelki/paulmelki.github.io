Hello! I'm a researcher working on different aspects of machine learning reliability (uncertainty quantification, robustness and generalization, explainability), applied statistics and probability for applications in agriculture and environmental sciences.

I'm currently a Research & Innovation Engineer at <a href="https://exxact-robotics.com/en" target="_blank">Exxact Robotics</a>, a fast growing agri-tech enterprise based in Épernay and Lyon (France).

Previously, I conducted my [PhD CIFRE on conformal prediction for computer vision-based decision-making in agriculture](https://theses.hal.science/tel-05226283) at the Signal & Image Processing group of the <a href="https://www.ims-bordeaux.fr/" target="_blank">IMS Lab</a> at the University of Bordeaux, in collaboration with Exxact Robotics. 

Before that, I obtained my Master's degree in Statistics & Econometrics at the prestigious <a href="https://www.tse-fr.eu/" target="_blank">Toulouse School of Economics</a>, and my Bachelor's degree with a major in Computer Science and a minor in Economics at the <a href="https://www.balamand.edu.lb/home/Pages/default.aspx" target="_blank">University of Balamand</a> in my native country of Lebanon.

I'm also interested in classical literature and arts (cinema, music, photography, architecture), trail running and hiking, philosophy (of science, ethics, metaphysics), as well as in the history of statistics and probability.
